First public beta of Glimt, a native, local, read-only file inspector for macOS 15 or later. Glimt
shows source code, Markdown and structured data through one engine in the host app, in Quick Look
previews and in Finder thumbnails. Files are never executed or changed, and the app does not use
the network.

## Highlights

- Syntax-highlighted Source, Raw and Hex views for a large catalog of programming, markup,
  configuration and template languages.
- Rendered Markdown (including TextBundle packages) and native Mermaid diagrams.
- Tree, Pretty and Table views for JSON, YAML, XML, property lists, CSV/TSV, logs, iCalendar, vCard,
  SBOMs, certificates, SQLite schemas and ZIP/TAR inventories (archives are never extracted).
- Quick Look Preview and Thumbnail extensions that share the app's settings.
- Formats & Views (⌘1) for view order, defaults and custom filename/extension mappings, and the
  Inquiry window for detected type, inspector and diagnostics.

The full inventory is in the [format masterlist](https://github.com/HansJoakimPersson/Glimt/blob/main/docs/formats/README.md).

## Known limitations

- **Not notarized.** This beta is ad-hoc signed, without an Apple Developer ID, so Gatekeeper
  blocks the first launch. Follow the installation steps below.
- **Finder provider selection is unverified.** macOS chooses one Quick Look provider per file type.
  Another installed app may win even when Glimt renders the file in its own window. `.ts` files are
  a known conflict (macOS may classify them as MPEG transport streams).
- Beta identifiers and settings formats may still change before 1.0. Settings are kept in
  `~/Library/Application Support/Glimt/`.
- There is no automatic update; check the [releases page](https://github.com/HansJoakimPersson/Glimt/releases).
- Graphviz DOT is shown as source only; graph drawing is deferred.

## Install

1. Download `Glimt-0.1.0-beta.1-macOS.zip` and, optionally, verify it against the checksum below:
   `shasum -a 256 -c Glimt-0.1.0-beta.1-SHA256SUMS.txt`
2. Unzip it and move `Glimt.app` to `/Applications`.
3. Open Glimt. macOS reports that it cannot verify the app: choose **Done**, then open
   **System Settings › Privacy & Security** and click **Open Anyway** for Glimt.
   Alternatively, remove the download quarantine in Terminal:
   `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Glimt.app`
4. Launch Glimt once so macOS registers its extensions, then check that Glimt is enabled under
   **System Settings › General › Login Items & Extensions › Quick Look**.
5. `Glimt › About Glimt` shows the installed version: **0.1.0 Beta 1 (1)**.

To uninstall, quit Glimt and move it to the Trash. Settings and logs remain under
`~/Library/Application Support/Glimt/` and `~/Library/Logs/Glimt/` until you remove them.
